How the forecast works

Sources. Deals from the CRM; quotas, stage probabilities and the MRR plan from Google Sheets; MRR movements from the warehouse.

Weighted forecast = closed won + each open deal's amount × its stage probability, counting deals whose expected close date falls in the period.

Forecast categories come from the stage: commit and best case are the deals leadership can count on most.

Year to date runs through the last full month, so a part-month never drags attainment down. Change a probability in the sheet and every forecast on this page follows.
